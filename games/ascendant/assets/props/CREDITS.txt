ASCENDANT - prop asset credits
==============================================================================

Every .glb in assets/props/<theme>/ was derived from a pack already vendored in
forgeflow-games/pipeline/assets/3d-models/. Source packs:

  [FP] Synty Fantasy Props (fantasy-props)
  [MV] Synty Medieval Village MegaKit (medieval-village)
  [PP] Poly Pizza community models (polypizza)

glTF sources ([FP], [MV]) were re-baked to self-contained .glb by
_spec-side tooling: node/mesh transforms flattened, the shared 2-7 MB Synty trim
atlas sampled into per-vertex COLOR_0, and roughness/metalness taken from the
median of the ORM map. No textures are embedded, so each prop is a few tens of
kB instead of several MB. [PP] .glb files were copied verbatim.
