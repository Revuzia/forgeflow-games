How to Play

  • Campaign — a five-realm story. Each realm has 5 levels (~4–8 waves each). A named Realm Warden with signature attacks ends level 5 — expect a few words before the fight.
  • PVP Arena — three rival-champion modes: Duel of Champions (best of 3), Ember Rush (score race), Last Sanctum (survival gauntlet). Rival is AI today; online rivals next.
  • Class cards — champions emote (dance/wave/cheer). Drag a card preview to rotate; double-click cycles emotes.
  • Basic attack is Left Click only (Space/J also work). Short cadence, no key 1.
  • Three classes — Crucible Knight, Storm Adept, Umbral Ranger. Same layout: LMB + 1 / 2 / 3 skills + Shift phase.
  • Crucible Knight stances — press V (or Tab) to swap Two-Handed ↔ Sword & Shield.
  • 1 · 2 · 3 cast class skills (with SFX). Cooldown shows time on each button. Shift = dodge/phase.
  • Camera — scroll zooms; RMB drag looks left/right and up/down. Invert Y in Settings if needed.
  • Audio — battle music + skill SFX. Volumes in Settings.
  • F3 toggles the debug overlay (FPS, wave/level, enemies).
  • Waves never pause — clear a pack and keep moving.
