EMBER-1 "MAGMA WORKS" — what I actually did, in order.

I drove the shipped page headless at ?dev=1&quality=low&autoscale=0 through
_harness/_play_e1.py + _play_e1b.py (thirteen scripted segments, real
KeyboardEvents through window, player.__test.state() sampled every 110-250 ms,
119 PNGs into _shots/play_ember-1/). Every segment clicked NEW GAME on the real
title, called __dev.unlockAll(), then entered ember-1 through the real loader at
a named checkpoint. Teleports were used only to travel; every station was then
walked on foot.

BEAT 1 — THE SHORE. Spawned at (0, 4.60, 45) on the yard flat. Read the MAGMA
WORKS board (legible from the pad and from 3 m). Walked the swept track north
picking up the trail coins, then off-track west to (-8.4, 3.06, 35.9) to look at
the shore and the lake edge, then back to the quay.

BEAT 2 — THE QUAY AND THE FLAME CATWALK. Took cp-quay. Looked at the quay
pedestal and the race-start board. Stood at the catwalk mouth (z 12 and z 10.6)
and forced the vent cycle to clock 0.2 / 1.6 / 2.9 / 3.6 and to a firing/idle
pair, photographing each. Walked straight into vent 1 with the clock at 0 and
died at clock 0.70 (deaths 0 -> 1) - the warn window held all the way to the
jet. Froze the hazards and pushed north from twelve different z positions to
find out whether the vent props block the deck (they do not), then queried the
broadphase: four colliders in the whole catwalk band, none of them a vent.
Finally ran the spine three times from the quay at clock 1.5 / 2.6 / 3.4 —
crossed once, died twice, both times running into a jet already lit. Stood
still at (0, 3.00, 0) for nine seconds between vents 2 and 3 with the bumbler
patrolling: nothing.

BEAT 2 (cont.) — THE JUNCTION AND THE IRON HAT. Took cp-junction. Read the
raft board. Walked on to the METAL HAT at (-4, 3.90, -9): power flipped from
null to metal, helmet on, 20 s HUD timer.

BEAT 3 — THE RAFT FIELD. Four attempts. Two raft-by-raft hops from (0, -11)
and two continuous run-and-jump crossings from (0, -12.4). All four ended in

and let it take me down: it sank and INCINERATED me. A fifth attempt from the

BEAT 5 — THE EAST GANTRY (ROUTE B). Stood at the gantry head at (40, 6, 16),
forced both pulse beams through their cycle, then ran the 25 m from z 15 to

-34) and played the authored climb — one jump plus four alternating wall kicks —

BEAT 9 — THE SLAG PIT. Crossed the chain bridge from (-16, 6.6, -34), walked
