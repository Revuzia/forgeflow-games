Loudness report (live capture)

Records what the module actually sends to your speakers (a tap after the compressor, limiter and mute, at the current volumes) while it plays each sound, so the numbers are what you hear. Run it with the sliders at their defaults (music 0.70, sfx 0.80). It plays in real time: about 3 minutes for all sfx, 1.5 minutes for layers, 4 minutes for music. Peak is the largest sample; RMS is over the part that is not silent (sfx) or the steady part (layers, music). An OfflineAudioContext render is deliberately not used: measured in Chromium it came out several dB quieter than the live output for loud sounds (jump: about -31 dBFS offline against -23 live) while agreeing for quiet ones (the form layers), so it cannot be used to balance sounds against each other. The cause was not established.
