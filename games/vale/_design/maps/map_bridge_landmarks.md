# Needlespan (`map_bridge`): landmark and art brief

For the Blender map artist (`art/blender/maps/map_bridge.py` → `art/out/maps/map_bridge/scene.glb`, `minimap.png`).
- **Layout truth:** `content/maps/map_bridge.json`. It is generated by `_design/tools/map_gen.py` and checked in the real sim by `_harness/probe_maps.ts`.
- **Preview:** `_design/maps/map_bridge.png`.
- **Look law:** `_design/STYLE_BIBLE.md` and `_design/tokens.json` `grade.maps.bridge`.
- **Names:** `_design/WORLD.md` §3.
- **Coordinates:** sim `(x, y)`, with x running west (Aubade, screen-left) → east (Serenade, screen-right) and y running north → south. Three.js is `(x, height, y)` and Blender is **`(x, −y, height)`**. All walkable ground is flat at height 0.

## 1. The map in one paragraph

Needlespan is the needle's fallen shaft lying across a gorge. One straight road, the **Span Road** (`road_span`, 122 m gate to gate), runs along the shaft's flat top.
- **Size.** The walkable top is 14 m wide, so the whole width fits in one screen (17.3 m tall).
- **Ends.** It leaves from the **Dawn Arch** terrace (Aubade, west) and reaches the **Lamp Gate** terrace (Serenade, east).
- **The Snap.** At the middle is the round plaza where the shaft cracked, 24 m across, with two broken stubs standing on the crack line.
- **Walls.** Everything that is not the shaft or a terrace is **the gorge**. The walls in the JSON are the gorge's edges: they must render as a **drop**, never as cliffs rising from the span (`art.terrain.wallStyle: "gorge"`).
- **Symmetry.** The map is mirror-identical across x = 78.

## 4. Terrain materials (walkable roughness ≥ 0.5)

| Surface | Where | Material | Value (L*) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deck (the lane) | the shaft top, y 15–29 | honed needle stone in long courses running along the shaft | 60–66 |
| Inscription band | a 0.4 m band on **each walkable edge** of the deck, terraces and Snap | carved hour-script in chalk inlay, so the edge of the drop is unmistakable | 70 |
| Terraces | discs r 13 at both ends | Aubade: chalk flags with dawnglass rings round the fountain. Serenade: ochre sandstone with lampresin inlay | 60–66 |
| The Snap | disc r 12 | the same stone, crazed with fine cracks radiating from the crack line | 60–66, cracks ≤ 6 L* |
| Gorge walls (edges) | `walls[]` | the shaft's flank curving down, then raw rock to the fog | 15–30 |
| Needlegrass | `thickets[]`: the flank ledges (x 46 and 110) and the Snap's four corners | stone-green blades growing from the cracks, on a carved curb | 48–54 |

- **Light.** Sun 46° elevation, bearing 200° (`#FFC890`, 2.9). sunDir (−0.2376, 0.7193, 0.6528). Ambient 0.7.
- **Temperature.** The Dawn Arch end may lean cool and the Lamp Gate end warm, through materials only. There is no team hue anywhere in the world.

## 6. Minimap (`minimap.png`, shown at 384 × 96 px, about 2.2 px/m)

The map is a white bar on black. Paint it flat:
- **Deck and terraces:** L* 75.
- **Snap:** the same value, with the crack as a 1 px darker line.
- **Bands and stubs:** wall-dark notches.
- **Gorge:** L* 12.

Structures and Sunmotes are drawn live by the UI. Readability comes from the bar's silhouette alone: the two terraces, the two pinches per half, and the round Snap.
