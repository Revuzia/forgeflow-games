- **Cause**: `shape.ts flatPoints()` pulls every point beyond the cut plane onto the plane (and rescales to the rest volume) but never re-centres the result. The shape-matching goal is `GOAL_i = centroid(particles) + R * QG_i` (softbody.ts substep), so a goal whose own mass-weighted centroid is off the origin pulls the WHOLE body by (K x offset) every substep with no reaction: a thrust, for as long as the flat face lasts (roundTau 2 s; putty 6 s). Face down: the goal's centroid is above the origin, the body is pulled up; face up: pulled into the table (no launch). (The sideways case is not shown to launch anything: Split in two was unchanged by this fix, see the second cause below.) The thrust grows with the family's stiffness (K = w2 / (1 + w2)), which is why only firm silicone, pop dome, putty, gummy, mochi, slow-rise and bead families launch.
