## Findings

No MAJOR. Severities follow the VERIFY.md definition.

- **F1 (MINOR): a Shift pull dragged straight DOWN from the lower half pays 0.5 SP instead of about 2.18.** The pay is read from the real hand only (the mirrored hand's grab and snap are `twin`, skipped by the meter); with a Shift pair the real hand's target is floor-clamped (`SHIFT_FLOOR_Y`), so its pull level is 0.26 to 0.34 while the mirrored hand (going up) reaches 1.0 and the body is visibly stretched to its maximum. Numbers: at -90 degrees 34 of 50 species pay exactly 0.500 SP (the other 16 pay up to 2.181), at -60 and -30 all 50 pay 2.148 .. 2.192; with the clamp removed -90 pays a median 2.181. Repro: `node _v/phys.ts one dollop -90 shift 1.8` prints `"sp":0.5` and `"snaps":[{"finger":0,"I":0.264},{"finger":1,"I":1}]`; `... dollop -60 shift 1.8` prints `"sp":2.1808`. Suggested fix (shell, not applied): pay from the stronger of the two hands (feed the snap with the larger intensity), or leave the real hand unclamped and clamp only the mirror. The engineer's decision 2 says the clamp "only matters for a press near the top of the dome or near the centre"; it also matters for presses on the lower half.
- **F2 (MINOR): Shift pulls cannot stretch both sides along the vertical.** Both hands reach pull level >= 0.98 in 50/50 runs at 0 and 180 degrees, 50/50 at -135, 41/50 at 45, but **0/49 straight up (90) and 0/50 straight down (-90)**: the lower hand is pinned at 2 cm above the table (min-hand pull level median 0.24 at 90, 0.30 at -90). Two real fingers reach both at 45 of 45 (and invert meshes in 15 of 286, the reason for the clamp). Expected on a table (nothing can be pulled through it) but the promise "pulls both sides" holds only for non-vertical drags; the hint does not say so.
- **F3 (MINOR): two-handed pulls have sustained creases, equal to two real fingers, worse than a lone pull.** Dihedral between adjacent triangle normals, measured with the physics lane's own fold definition (settled rest shape reads 50.4 degrees, as in `probe_softbody.ts`). Frames over 120 degrees, at least 10 of them in a run: Shift 101 of 299 (34%) at reach 1.8 and 22 of 100 (22%) at 0.9; two real touches 111 of 286 (39%) and 20 of 100 (20%); lone finger 7 of 100 (7%) at 0.9. At 0.9, paired against two touches at the same two points, Shift's worst crease is higher in median by 10 degrees (p95 +30, max +58; worse by over 20 degrees in 20 of 100 pairs). 14 of 299 Shift runs are still creased over 60 degrees 3 s after release (kneadle, thudge, cindergoo, fossilo; two touches 21 of 286, up to 179.5; lone pull 6 of 100 at 0.9): the plastic-memory families. 35 of 50 species have at least one sustained case among the 6 directions: twangle 6 of 6, wrigglo 5, 14 others 4. It is the physics lane's known open issue; Shift just makes it reachable with one mouse. In the real browser the five worst species looked clean (see item 4), so it is a number, not a seen defect at the default camera. The old verdict's "2/612" bar is for lone pulls; no accepted bar exists for two-handed pulls.
- **F4 (MINOR): float mode (G, gravity off) is not covered by the floor clamp and Shift pulls invert meshes there.** By design the clamp is off without a table; measured 9 of 199 Shift runs with volume outside 0.8..1.25 (wrigglo@90 -0.81 .. 10.1, wrigglo@-90 -0.53 .. 16.7, twangle, tadpolo, gloopsy, cindergoo, glimglop), two touches 5 of 189, lone finger 5 of 199. Repro: `node _v/phys.ts one wrigglo 90 shift 1.8 float` prints volume -0.81 .. 10.07. Not caused by Shift (the same species invert under a lone finger) but the engineer's "0 of 38 inverted" holds only on a table.
- **F5 (MINOR, tooling): `snap.mjs` drops every `dist` folder including `node_modules/vite/dist`**, so the snapshot it makes cannot run `tsc` or vite. Workaround used: copy of `src` / `_harness` plus a junction (below).
- **N1 (NOTE, pre-existing, not Shift, owner may want it): a LONE finger dragged down from the lower half past the limit inverts the mesh for some species.** `plain` at -60: 13 of 50 species outside 0.8..1.25 (negative volume: twangle -5.3, wrigglo -1.0, gloopsy -23.6); at -90: 16 of 50 (twangle -12.7, tadpolo -5.0, glimglop -0.3). Repro: `node _v/phys.ts one twangle -60 plain 1.8` prints volume -5.26 .. 20.37, `carried: true`. The Shift clamp protects Shift on a table; a lone finger (touch or mouse) has no such protection. Physics lane.
- **N2 (NOTE): the mirrored hand is pulled further than the real one.** With the LIVE centre the reflected target moves as the body's centre of mass sinks and slides: in 52 of 249 non-vertical Shift runs the two hands end more than 0.1 pull level apart (dollop at 0.9: 0.738 vs 0.834); two real fingers end equal (0.738 vs 0.734). The target is exactly the reflection of the pointer (6e-13 px), so this is the physics reacting, plus the engineer's known shrink effect. Pay unaffected.
- **N3 (NOTE, design): Shift pressed after the drag has begun does nothing** (verified in the real browser: one side). The mode is read once at the commit move.
- **N4 (NOTE): the verdict is for an uncommitted tree** (hash above); and the engineer's browser rows did not check console / network errors, mine did (0).

## After reading the engineer's report (`INPUT_SHIFT.md`), second pass
- Its claims that I re-measured and confirm: differential 0 differences (its 25,000 scripts vs my 140,000 + driver layer); both grabs released once each; one voice, one release, one payment (its 2.2693 vs 2.2693; my pay matrix: identical distributions); no lift for Shift; touch never mirrors; the floor clamp stops the inversions (its 3 of 38 -> 0 of 38; mine 19 of 299 -> 0 of 299); two-handed creases equal two fingers (its 29 of 38 fold > 120 vs two touches 24 of 38; mine 39% / 50%); hint text on desktop only.
- What its report did not test and I did: all 50 species and 6 directions (it used 4 to 6 species at 7 press points, horizontal drag for the real-game rows) -> F1, F2; float mode -> F4; the lone-finger downward inversion -> N1; leave paths in the middle of a Shift pull on the real facade (45 of 46); the hint and Settings line fit at five desktop sizes; console and page errors (0).

- Its own numbers are consistent with mine; I found no claim in it that my measurements contradict.
