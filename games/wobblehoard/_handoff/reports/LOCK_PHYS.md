## 2. Slots, locks (done)
- 5 `Finger`s; grab slots 0, 1 as ever, slots 2 to 4 and the 6 lock places are a shared `IDLE_GRAB` until first used (a body that never locks / has no touch finger allocates nothing more). Every finger / grab loop is per slot (pinch share = any other finger down, pinch overlap for every pair, volume blocking, fold re-seat, collisions, press events, metrics.fingers 0..5, strands).
- `lockGrab(slot)`: false (nothing changes) for no grab / bad slot (-1, 1.5, 5, NaN, Infinity, undefined) / carried body / pull level < 0.95 / `lockN >= maxLocks` (public field, default 6; the SHELL sets 4 on low quality or checks `metrics.locked` first). On success the patch and its eased target move to a lock, the slot is freed WITHOUT a snap (locking pays nothing), the feet stay glued, the hold time keeps counting; `metrics.locked` / `grabbed` update at once, not at the next step.
- `releaseLock(i | 'all')`: one 'snap' per lock, heldFor = the whole hold since its grab, intensity = the pull level, finger = the slot it was grabbed with (the mouse: 0), NOT rate limited (5 locks released together pay 5). `lockAt(p)`: nearest locked patch vertex within 0.4 R, else -1, no allocation. `metrics.pull` / `grabbed` stay "a pointer grab" (locks excluded).
- twoHanded latch counts locks as hands (a slot with finger and grab counts once); with a lock, a second grab asked to 1.9 x maxPull never lifts the body; after every lock and finger is up the lone pick-up lifts again; a lock while carried is refused. warmUp() runs the lock paths and slots 2/3 on its twin (the body stays bit-identical: hash before = after, measured).
