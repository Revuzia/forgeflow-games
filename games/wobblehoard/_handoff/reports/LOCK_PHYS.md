## Step 2: 5 slots + locks in softbody.ts (DONE; see measured below)
- fingers: 5 `Finger`s (cheap, eager); grabs: slots 0, 1 as ever, slots 2 to 4 are `IDLE_GRAB` until first used (a real `Grab(n)` is made on the first `grab(2..4)`); locks: 6 places, a real `Grab(n)` made on the first lock in that place. A body that never locks and has no touch fingers allocates nothing more than before.
- every finger / grab loop is per slot (pinch share = any other finger down; pinch overlap for every pair; blocking weights, fold re-seat, collisions, press events, metrics.fingers 0..5, strands); the code for fingers 0 and 1 is unchanged.
- `lockGrab(slot)`: refused (false, nothing changes) when there is no grab on the slot, the body is carried, the pull level is < 0.95 (`LOCK_MIN_PULL`, the level 'snap' would report), or `lockN >= maxLocks` (public `maxLocks`, default 6; the shell sets 4 on low quality, or checks `metrics.locked`). On success the grab's patch + eased target move to a free lock, the grab slot is freed WITHOUT a snap, the feet stay pinned, hold time keeps counting.
- `releaseLock(i | 'all')`: one 'snap' per lock, `heldFor` = whole hold since its grab, `intensity` = pull level, `finger` = the slot the lock was grabbed with; NOT rate limited (5 locks released together pay 5 pulls); the pinned feet's hold timer starts when the last grab and lock are gone.
- `lockAt(point)`: nearest locked patch vertex within 0.4 rest radii, else -1; no allocation. `metrics.locked` = count. `metrics.grabbed` stays "a pointer grab is active" (locks excluded); `metrics.pull` is the deepest ACTIVE GRAB (locks excluded), so the shell's "fully stretched?" reads the grab being pulled.
- twoHanded latch: counts locks as hands (a slot with a finger and a grab counts once); a lone grab beside one lock is never picked up; a lock refuses while carried.
- Measured: bit-identical to the pre-edit solver for 2-slot use: 8 species x (2 fingers pinch, grab + release, lone pull with carry + toss, two grabs, float) = 8/8 identical stateHash series (260 samples each).
