## Findings
- **M1 (MAJOR) U03: one swipe across several tips is refused or makes too few pieces.** The engineers' own `node _harness/shellview/cutter_checks.ts --only=u03 --v [--full]` FAILS on the snapshot ("0 species made 3 or more pieces, most 0" on 8 species; most 2 on 16). 13 of 16 species answer "That would make a piece too thin. Try another angle." (0 pieces: granulo, petalop, burrbin, marigel, rattlebead, caromel, tidelume, glimglop, somnuff, fossilo, skeinara, constello, prismelo); hushpuff / taffelin / cindergoo make 2 pieces (0.9989 + 0.0011, 0.9851 + 0.0149, 0.9823 + 0.0177) where the independent count of parts is 3. My own search (`t_multi.mjs`, smaller parts each >= 12 vertices, about 2 % of the mesh) is refused the same way on granulo, petalop, taffelin, tidelume, glimglop; on burrbin, marigel, rattlebead, cindergoo, caromel only 5 to 9 vertex slivers exist and those are refused too. Real mouse, granulo 960x600: "U03: ONE swipe across several tips makes 3 pieces (made 1, lastParts 2)" FAIL (the dev line starts on the wordmark, so that run is also a UI issue). Single-tip swipes work (about 72 across the 21 species, 0 refused). CUT3 2.3 says refusal remains only for a sliver the mesh cannot hold; here it is the common case.
- **m1 (MINOR) U02 top tips do not fall to the table at once.** granulo tip 1 (the first, top tip): lands on its own stump, reaches the table at 7.70 s (node) and is still creeping 0.21 m/s at 6 s in the real browser (`br_gate.mjs --scene=a --vp=960`: "rest NOT in 6 s, peak windowed speed 0.214 m/s"); rests by 14 s. ambrosel tips 2, 3 reach the table at 10.8 and 11.1 s, diademo 2, 3 at 6.5 and 6.8 s; 9 of 41 tips in the 14 s run rest ON the star 0.1 to 1.1 m up (ambrosel 1, glimglop 2, somnuff 3, constello 1 and 4, prismelo 1 and 4, diademo 1 and 4). No launch anywhere. Picture `scratchpad/integration_c/shots/a_960_2_tip_at_rest.png`: flat-topped star, the tip is a sparkle patch on the face.
- **m2 (MINOR) neck swell over the x1.25 bar:** wrigglo (slimegoo) Split in two x1.267 and vertical swipe x1.267 (top 0.757 -> 0.959 m); twangle vertical x1.218. Repro `node --no-warnings t_swell.mjs --sp=wrigglo`.
