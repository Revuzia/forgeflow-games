## What was wrong, and the fix at the root
**A-F5 / B-F1 / A-F6 / B-F2 (one cause).** `src/input/pointer.ts` forwards a `pointermove` to the game only while `app.input.isDown(id)`, and `game.ts` answered that for the Hand's gesture machine alone (`gestures.isActive`). In the Cut tool the press is taken by the blade, in Snap by `snapDrag`, so every move became a hover: Snap never turned or zoomed and the blade line stayed a dot at its start. The hook-driven checks (and 124/124, 183/183, 473/473) call `game.input` directly and bypass `pointer.ts`. Fix (`game.ts`): `isDown` is true for every owner of a pressed pointer: the gesture machine, the Cut blade, a Snap drag, and a held capsule (`Capsules.holds(id)`, added; the capsule's own `moved` slop never saw real moves either). A left mouse button in Snap still owns nothing (owner decision), so it stays a hover.
**A-F4.** `releaseEverything()` now clears the blade (and emits `blade` null) and Snap's drags, so a hidden tab, a panel or a ceremony cannot leave a half-made swipe.
**A-F2.** `cut.ts` joined any piece with a finger on it whose skin was within `TOUCH_GAP` 0.03 m of another for 0.4 s (or on a let-go). Pieces rest 0.16 m apart for 0.8 s and then close up on their own to touching (27 of 50 species under 0.03 m at 4 s; Dollop 0.029 m), so every touch joined them. The engineer's "does not join" row held the finger on a piece 0.105 m from its twin, and the "joins by dragging" row pressed the OUTER shoulder and dragged inward: the gesture machine classifies that as a RUB (a pull must go outward), the piece never moved, and the join came from the pieces resting together: the rows could only pass because of the bug. Fix: a contact is now STARTED only by a drag: `driver.touch.dragOf(out)` (new: the hand of a real finger's grab less where it began; a Shift pull's mirrored hand is not a finger) and `cut.ts` `DRAG_TOWARD_K = 0.25`: the pull must have moved the hand at least a quarter of the piece's radius toward the other piece. A tap, a press, a squeeze, a rub, a pull away or across never starts one; once running, a contact is kept by the old hysteresis (`TOUCH_KEEP_GAP`), so the 0.4 s hold and the let-go join are unchanged. CUT.md 1 says exactly this ("drag a piece into another and hold ... or let go while they touch").
**A-F1.** Split in two now looks for the plane that halves the piece best (`halving()` in `cut.ts`: a bisection on `measureCut` within 1.3 radii of the centre) when the middle plane would leave a part under 1/8, because a plane through a lopsided piece's centre of mass does not halve it. A new `Cutter.canSplit` (limit not reached and some piece at least 1/4 of the whole: a smaller one cannot be cut at all) disables the button with the line "Every piece is too small to split in two. Swipe near an edge to peel a small one off.", and the refusal the button gives says "No piece is big enough to split in two..." instead of advice it cannot follow.
**B-U1.** On a short landscape screen (height <= 520 px) the Cut bar and the Snap bar dock at the left edge, vertically centred, 176 px wide (142 px under 360 px of height, where the instruction paragraph also goes); on the narrowest portrait phones (<= 380 px wide) both keep to one row of buttons / one row of swatches and lose the paragraph. The tint names stay in the accessible name (visually hidden, not `display: none`). CSS only (`hoard.css`).
**B-U2.** `hoard.css`: `.meter-state` is hidden while the tray is open or the Cut tool is in hand. **B-U3 / B-U4 / B-M2.** `createToyTray(..., { canOpen })`: not during a ceremony (running or pending), not under Settings or the Hoard, not in Snap; a reveal that starts closes an open tray; a Toys click closes Settings first, as the Hoard button does. **B-M1.** `settingsPanel.ts`. **B-M3.** `snapBar.ts` `setSaving` uses `aria-disabled` (the shell's `saving` guard stays; `.cta-btn[aria-disabled]` is styled like `:disabled`). **B-M4.** `hudBinding.ts`: the Reconnect all button says "Whole again." when Skip animations made it instant (leave paths stay silent).
