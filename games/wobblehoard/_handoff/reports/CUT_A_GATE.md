## 1. Plain verdict for the owner
**The cut itself is what you asked for. What happens right after the cut is not yet.**
- Every cut gives two real halves: a flat, pale, glassy cut face, the eyes stay on the side they sit (between the eyes: one whole eye each; through an eye: that bead is cut in half, one half on each piece), no little ball, nothing is lost. Minis gives two complete minis with two eyes each. Reconnect all returns the exact original.
- **Not yet right (the physics contact, task P5, is not in this slice):** after Split in two the gel (dollop) climbs: one half hops 0.7 m and ends sitting ON the other; foam (capnap) and putty (kneadle) launch 0.4 to 0.6 m at 1.4 to 2.3 m/s. After a horizontal swipe the upper piece slides off and lands 0.9 to 1.3 m away at 1.5 to 2.6 m/s on all four materials. A swipe between the eyes throws the halves apart at up to 2.5 m/s. A swipe through an eye is calm (0.7 m/s). Nine of the 12 families are calm on Split in two (section 3).
- One thing was glue and is fixed: **Reconnect all lifted a piece 0.6 m into the air on the way home** (the clean style never ghosted the giver, so the shipped contact shoved the flush faces); now both stay on the table, meet mated and the whole appears at 1.5 to 1.6 s.
- Gate result: **not passed as is**. The headline picture (Split in two between the eyes) is right on 9 of 12 families and wrong on dollop, capnap, kneadle.
