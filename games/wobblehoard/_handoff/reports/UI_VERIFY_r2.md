## G. glyph counter rule (K14 / UI.md 5)
Game's own `jelly()`, white cell, DPR 1, pure-white enclosed region, inscribed diameter, 5 tones: cap 24 B and 8 lower counter 1 px (< 2); cap 36 B and 8 = 1+1 px, P R 4 6 9 0 = 3 px (< 4); A 5, D 7, O 7, Q 5 pass; 11 failures with the luminance >= 180 variant. In `out/glyph_last.png` all 12 glyphs read, B and 8 are slits. The builders' sheet (outline silhouette, 4x) passes; real pixels do not. Kept MINOR by V2's precedent (legible, product works); a literal reading of K14 makes it a MAJOR: thin the body stroke / open B and 8, or re-specify the metric in writing (orchestrator or owner call).
