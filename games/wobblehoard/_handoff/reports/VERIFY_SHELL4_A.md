## Findings

- **F2 (MAJOR) After a cut, ANY touch on a piece reconnects it with the piece beside it: pieces cannot be poked, squished or pulled once they have drifted together (about 3 s after the cut).** CUT.md section 1: "Play with the pieces ... poke, squish, pull, pick up and toss them" and "Reconnect: drag a piece into another and hold them together for about 0.4 s, or let go while they touch". `src/shell/cut.ts` `update()` (the touch-to-join block, lines 551 to 576) joins any piece with a finger on it (a press or a pull) whose skin is within `TOUCH_GAP` = 0.03 m of another piece's skin for `JOIN_HOLD_S` = 0.4 s, or when the finger lets go while they touch. It assumes pieces only touch when the player drags them together. They do not: pieces are parted to a 0.16 m gap for `SETTLE_S` = 0.8 s and then let go, and they close up again: the surface gap of the two halves of Dollop is 0.120 m at 1 s, 0.080 m at 2 s, 0.028 m at 3 s, 0.029 m at 4 s; across the 50 species 27 are under 0.03 m at 4 s.
  - Browser repro (real mouse, real stack): `scripts\repro_press_join.mjs` (Dollop, Split in two, Hand tool, 4 s rest, then ONE touch on the smaller piece), 5 trials, 5 joins: tap 60 ms -> joined on release (rejoin voice x1, 1 piece); press 150 ms -> joined on release; press 300 ms -> joined on release; squeeze 0.9 s with no movement -> the join began 0.45 s into the press with the finger still down; pull up and out 1.2 s -> the join began 0.45 s into the pull, before the piece was lifted.   - Node repro (the whole game core on the real SoftBody, all 50 species, `scripts\holdjoin.ts`, mouse): 4 s after a vertical Split in two, a 0.7 s press on the smaller piece started a join in **32 of 50** species and on the face piece in **31 of 50** (join at 417 ms in most).
