## Things you must know (honest list)
1. "Instantly when off screen" (LOCK.md rule 3) is NOT expressible: the seam has no instant flag and the sim is paused while hidden. I release at the return after > 60 s with the normal snap-back. Say if PHYS should add `releaseLock('all', true)`.
2. Leaving by body replacement (switch, `setGenome`, cut piece swap): the locks' snap events stay on the discarded body, so they pay nothing (same as any pull released that way today). Hoard, tools, hidden > 60 s keep the body: their snaps are drained and pay.
3. Shift locks ANY pull held (also a touch finger's, a laptop with a touch screen; a phone has no Shift). I did not exclude touch: it would need a flag on the `grab` action, which changes the action stream the pinned hash covers.
4. A press at the very tip of a stretched lobe can miss it (the ray passes the silhouette edge); anywhere on the upper ~30 % of the lobe finds the lock (`LOCK_REACH` 0.4 R). Real numbers in `real*.log`: hits at +180..255 px of a 340 px drag. The marker (the grabbed vertex) is on that silhouette edge.
5. Real-physics note for PHYS: pulls dragged into the table cannot reach pull level 0.95 (0.88 measured), so on a 5-point star standing on the table the two lower tips refuse the lock; that looks like the ground floor, not a bug, but the owner will see "3 of 5 locked".
6. Esc precedence: a revealed plate does not count as a panel (Esc releases locks first, then the plate/Menu as before).
7. The live-region and hint copy is mine; no audio file or sound was listened to (nobody can here).
