SQUISH KEEPER
An original game by ForgeFlow Games.
All sound is synthesised in your browser. All art is drawn in code.

Built with:
  three.js (MIT licence, the notice is below) draws the 3D jelly.
  Vite (MIT licence) builds the game.
  TypeScript (Apache-2.0 licence) is the language it is written in.
