## THE TWELVE

### 1. Swept-segment bullet collision — cover actually stops bullets
**Rationale:** At 60 fps ~85% of rounds sample past every wall and 100% pass through every ramp, so positioning, peeking and holding a building — the load-bearing BR mechanic — do not exist.
**Touch:** `runtime/3d/royale/weapons.js` → `testSegment` (endpoint containment test at `:486 if (c.kind !== "box") continue;`, terrain sample at `:525`, sub-step cap at `:432`), `crosshairPoint` aim march; `runtime/3d/royale/maps.js` → `losBlocked` (`:1110`) **must** ship in the same change or bots keep shooting through walls; new slab helper in `runtime/sim/royale.js` (pure, dual-export) consumed by all three; cases in `runtime/sim/royale.selftest.cjs`. Rewrite the grenade bounce normal (`weapons.js:492-503`) off the entry face — it currently derives from the overshot endpoint.
**Effort:** M (largest item here; the regression tail — prop destruction rate ~7×, bot lethality drop — is what makes it M, not the slab math)
**Accept:** Stand 5 m from a house wall on any map, empty an AR mag into it — zero damage numbers reach the far side and every shot leaves an impact FX on the wall face. Walk onto a tower interior ramp, fire down through it — rounds stop. A bot with LOS blocked by that same wall stops firing at you. Selftest green with a new "segment through a 0.32 m slab blocks at the entry face" assertion.

### 2. M opens the map **and** permanently mutes the game
**Rationale:** Two window listeners both own KeyM (`game_controls.js:218` toggleMute, `player.js:365` toggleBigMap); close the map with ESC and the game stays muted forever, across reloads, across every game on the portal origin.
**Touch:** `game_controls.js:218` — gate behind `CFG.mute_hotkey !== false`, mirroring the existing `fs_hotkey` pattern; set `window.GAME_CONFIG={…, mute_hotkey:false}` in `games/last-circle/index.html`. **Fix the generator too:** `pipeline/templates/shared/game_controls.js:175` and `pipeline/engine/runtime/game_controls.js:175` carry the identical line. Bump `?v=` on both script tags.
**Effort:** S
**Accept:** Press M mid-match → big map opens, gunfire/footsteps still audible. Close with ESC, fire a shot — audio still plays. Reload the page — still unmuted. Corner mute button still works.

### 3. Parachute A/D steering is mirrored
**Rationale:** The glide strafe basis is `(cos y, +sin y)` while every other axis in the file uses `(cos y, −sin y)` — the error is exactly 2·yaw, so holding D moves you *backwards* when facing ±X and turning the camera rotates the drift the wrong way at double rate, which reads as "the chute feels floaty" rather than "A/D is inverted."
**Touch:** `runtime/3d/royale/player.js:565` — negate the Z strafe term. Hoist `fwdRight(yaw)` into `runtime/sim/royale.js` (**not** weapons.js — the CommonJS selftest can only `require` the sim) and consume it from both `:564-565` and the ground branch `:662`. Add a perpendicularity assert to `royale.selftest.cjs` (it passes 46/46 with the bug in place today, so the selftest alone is not the acceptance gate).
**Effort:** S
**Accept:** In the drop phase, face roughly +X (use the compass), hold D — the canopy tracks right, and the direction stays right as you sweep the mouse. Repeat facing −Z. Land on a chosen DROP SELECT rooftop.
**Cut:** the S-brake (`glideF` floor at `:563`) is a separate feel tune — decide it on its own before/after check, don't ride it in.

### 4. Weapon swap fires a hybrid weapon and resets the fire-rate cooldown
**Rationale:** `stepWeapon` caches `const wpn = a.weapon;` (`:229`) *before* applying `inp.slot` (`:235`), so `def` at `:247` is the OLD weapon while `fire()` stamps the NEW id on the projectile — shotgun→sniper on a live fire edge spawns 9 pellets each resolving as 105 sniper damage against 200 EHP; separately `equipSlot` (`:177`) rebuilds the weapon with `cd: 0`, so tap-2-tap-1 double-pumps the shotgun.
**Touch:** `runtime/3d/royale/weapons.js` — hoist the `inp.slot` handling above `:229` (or re-read `a.weapon` after it); carry the pending cooldown on the slot in `equipSlot` (`:171-183`). Runtime invariant guard `def === K.WEAPONS[a.weapon.id]` goes in weapons.js, **not** the selftest (it can't load browser ESM).
**Effort:** S
**Accept:** Practice range — fire the shotgun, immediately tap 2 then 1, and the second shell will not fire until the full 0.857 s has elapsed. Swap shotgun→sniper while holding LMB: the kill feed, tracer, and damage number all report one weapon, and no 9-pellet 105-damage burst appears.
**Cut:** `drawS` per-class draw time — a real design addition, decide separately.

**Effort:** S
