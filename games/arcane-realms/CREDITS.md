# Arcane Realms TCG — Credits & Attribution

- **Game design, code, card set, balance**: ForgeFlow Games (built with Claude).
- **All artwork** (117 card illustrations, 11 token illustrations, battlefield,
  key art, card back, 6 hero portraits): generated via xAI image generation
  (grok-imagine-image / -quality) exclusively for this game, 2026-07-06.
  No stock assets, no reused assets from other ForgeFlow games.
- **Music & sound**: 100% procedural WebAudio synthesis written for this game
  (menu theme, two battle themes, victory/defeat stings, ~20 SFX). No samples.
- **Engine**: [three.js](https://threejs.org) r172 (MIT), vendored locally.
- **Fonts**: system Georgia/serif stack — no embedded webfonts.

No Wizards of the Coast or Blizzard assets, names, or text are used. Game
mechanics are independently implemented genre conventions.
