READING
- DRIFT: the control (run 6) ran 0.9 fps against 1.5 for run 1 on identical settings. The machine got slower
  over the 12 minutes, so the run-to-run noise (about ±0.6 fps) is larger than any single flag's effect.
  These single-flag A/Bs are NOT conclusive. Interleave repeats (e.g. ultra, X, ultra, X) to separate them.
- pagepool: with it off, metal buffer allocs roughly tripled (6.5 -> 17.8 MB/frame) and post main time rose (185 -> 353 ms).
  So pagepool does cut allocations. The fps effect is lost in the noise.
- shnear: off gave 1.6 vs 1.5/0.9 for ultra. No visible difference; no clear cost or benefit.
- fartrees: off cut tris 28M -> 11.8M, draws 1287 -> 872, patches built 2037 -> 276, main mean 494 -> 305 ms,
  but fps did not rise (1.1). GPU post time stays about 1.2–1.6 s, so the GPU-side post chain is the limit, not tree geometry.
- GPU cost on Ultra is scene (about 1.0 s) + post (about 1.2 s) per frame. High cuts scene to 242 ms and post to 588 ms
  and is the only variant above 3 fps. Main-thread time is mostly bufferSubData/bufferData in the shadows and post slots,
  with 50–70 uploads inside a pass per frame on Ultra and 245 on High.

EXTRA CHECKS
- Mouse drag at low fps: it PANS now. Two drags at about 1 fps moved the camera and the powers wheel did not open. Fixed.
- Speed keys: 9 = 1000x (shows "x946 time-lapse"), then 1 returns to x1.00. Works.
  Note: key 5 is 10x (shows x10.0), not 1000x as the protocol says. 5 then 1 also returns to x1.00.
- Known issues, all still visible:
  * White/grey full-screen frame while zooming through the cloud deck: yes, every run (about the 3rd zoom step).
  * Far trees bright/blocky: yes. Zoomed out (High run especially), the trees on the near hill turn into

  * Water covering the hills east of Rubolhin: yes, every run; the rippled lake surface with tree trunks showing through.
