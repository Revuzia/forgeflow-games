READING
1. Water only: wfast=3 IS faster, but this build is noisy here (the P repeats were 7.0 / 2.8 / 4.3).
   - In each P -> wfast=3 pair it is 1.7x (7.0 -> 11.6) and 2.3x (2.8 -> 6.4).
   - Against the P mean (~4.7) the wfast=3 mean (~9) is ~1.9x.
   - wfrag=0 (no water fragment shading) is 17.2.
   - wfast=3 with all six wshade3 parts off is 16.7, about the same as wfrag=0. So most of wfast=3's remaining cost is in those parts.
   - noreflect alone gives 11.9; wvert=0 adds nothing.
2. Full game: wfast=3 is within the noise of N.
   - N: 1.5 / 1.0 / 1.7 (mean 1.4). wfast=3: 1.4 / 1.7 (mean 1.55).
   - wfast=3&deepbed=1: 2.2. fogwater=0: 1.9.
   - The full game is limited by more than the water on this Mac.

LOOK CHECKS (N vs N + wfast=3, same scripted camera steps; pairs in genesis_round18_water_shots/)
a. Landing view by day (07:45-07:48): mostly better with wfast=3.
   - Same sea colour. wfast=3 shows clearer wave texture all the way to the horizon; normal water is softer and hazier there.
   - Palm reflections at the bank are bolder and more solid with wfast=3; normal water's are finer and grainier.
   - Foam: the thin white foam line at the beach edge is weaker with wfast=3; N shows small white bits.
   - Sun glint is similar.
   - Verdict: wfast=3 slightly better on waves and reflections, slightly worse on beach foam.
b. Lake/pond by a town (Taabon), by day. NOT a clean pair: the clock could not be stopped at the same hour (N at 08:43 morning, wfast=3 at 16:01 late afternoon).
   - Both show the pond reflecting the sky, and the sea beyond with sun glints / wave texture.
   - No obvious fault in either.
c. River: none found near the landing spot or the town.
d. Night (moon on the water; N 22:44, wfast=3 23:37): about EQUAL.
   - Both show moonlit sky reflections on the sea. wfast=3 waves are a touch crisper.
   Sunset: only wfast=3 landed at sunset (18:44): warm orange sun streaks on the sea, looks right. The N try stopped at 15:15, so there is no N sunset to compare.
e. Storm/rain: none in view. Rain fell at the landing in Part 3 earlier but not during these shots.
f. Orbit, zoomed all the way out: EQUAL. At full zoom-out the planet is a small dot among the stars, so the water can't be judged.
g. Panning on wfast=3 (frames between small pans): no shimmer or swimming seen.
   - But wfast=3 shows a STRAIGHT DIAGONAL SEAM on the sea in the lower-right corner: a hard line where the water's brightness and speckle change.
   - It is there in both pan frames and in the landing frame. The same spot on N has no line (crops cr_g_pan2*.jpg).
   - At ~1-2 fps true motion shimmer can't be judged.
h. N + fogwater=0: YES, the pale hill shape over the sea comes BACK (with the dark kelp "trunks" through it). See run15 shot vs run10.
